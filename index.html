Turn a data room full of filings into verified answers, reports, and memos.

Financial Document Intelligence ingests a virtual data room (VDR) of financial and legal documents, then produces three things: a grounded Q&A chatbot, on-demand visual reports, and a full investment-memo draft — with every generated number and claim checked against its source before it's shown. The system below is live: pick your own charts, watch a memo get drafted, ask real questions, and open the actual source filings to verify any of it yourself.

About this demo: the source corpus is Planet Fitness, Inc.'s public SEC EDGAR filings (10-Ks and proxy statements), used as a realistic stand-in for a confidential virtual data room — no non-public or client data is used anywhere in this demo. The chatbot is rate-limited per visitor; the memo shown is real output from the actual pipeline (drafted and verified in advance, not re-generated on every click).
